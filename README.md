An entry for the make.fun competition `u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253`
(Two-Sentence Horror).

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one horror
story in exactly two sentences (98 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The hospice nurse said my mother died peacefully at 3am, holding my hand. I didn't arrive until 4.

A peaceful deathbed, retold in the second sentence as something else
entirely: if I was not there at 3am, someone — or something — was holding
her hand in my place, and she died believing it was me. The horror is never
named; the reader fills in the worst of it. Restrained, concrete, and
original — no gore, no familiar twist.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
